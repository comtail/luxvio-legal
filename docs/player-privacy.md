## 2. Principle

LUXVIO Player works without a user account and without servers of the provider. The app contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking services and no crash reporting to third parties. The provider receives no personal data through the app.

## 3. Local processing on your TV

The app stores data only in its own storage on your TV: the sources you set up with their connection parameters and credentials, the "Recently played" list, playback positions and settings. Other apps cannot access this storage. All data is deleted when you uninstall the app. You can clear "Recently played" and the playback positions in the settings.

## 4. Connections to your own sources

The app only connects to devices and services you set up yourself (for example WebDAV, an HTTP folder or a DLNA media server) and to connected USB drives. The respective server receives the technically necessary connection data such as the IP address of the TV, the requested paths and – if required for sign-in – your credentials. How that server processes data is decided by its operator, usually you.

Unencrypted connections (HTTP) can expose connection and sign-in data to others on the same network. Outside your home network, use HTTPS where possible.

## 5. Trial and purchase (Samsung Checkout)

All features are free for 7 days from the first start. The app stores the start of the trial only on your TV. You buy the full version through Samsung Checkout with your Samsung account; Samsung processes the payment data and the provider receives none. To verify and restore a purchase, the app sends the ID of your Samsung account, the country of the TV and the purchase number to Samsung's purchase interface. Locally the app only stores whether the full version has been bought.

## 6. Samsung

Samsung's privacy notice applies to downloads, updates and the operation of the TV: https://www.samsung.com/uk/info/privacy/

## 7. Legal bases

Where processing is attributable to the provider, it serves to provide the functions you request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Technically necessary access to your device is based on § 25(2) TDDDG.

## 8. Your rights

You have the rights under Art. 15 to 21 GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, objection). Contact: info@corevant.de. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (BayLDA).

## 9. Changes

This policy is updated when the app changes significantly. The current version is available in the app settings.
